## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added

- The ai-use-register skill: scope, scan, group, interview, assess, validate, report.
- A static scanner that records evidence of AI use with file, line and quote, deterministically.
- A register validator with 24 rules, including evidence quotes checked against the files.
- A deterministic Markdown register report.
- A worked example for a fictional organisation: scan output, register and report.
- Installers for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Cursor, with generated adapters.
- Community files, citation metadata and a Dependabot configuration.
