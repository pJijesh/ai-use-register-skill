# Interviewing the owner

Ask one question at a time. If the answer is "I do not know", record it as unknown and move on; do not guess.

For each system, in this order:

1. Who owns this system, by role and name, and who would be told if it went wrong?
2. What is it for, in one sentence, and what decision or action does it influence?
3. Who is affected by it: staff, customers, job candidates, the public? Are they told it is AI?
4. What data goes in? Does any of it identify a person? Could it include health, ethnicity, religion, union membership, biometrics or other special category data, even by accident?
5. Does the data leave the organisation, and under which contract or terms?
6. What does the system do on its own, and what does a person check before it takes effect?
7. How is it tested, monitored and corrected? Who can switch it off?
8. When did anyone last review it, and when is the next review?
9. Is it a pilot, in production, or retired?

Stop early if the owner is not the right person; ask who is.

Record answers in the owner's words where they matter (purpose, oversight), and mark anything you inferred from code as inferred.
